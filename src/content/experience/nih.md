I currently work in NHLBI's Division of Intramural Research, collaborating with the 70 clinical labs, basic science labs, and core facilities in the DIR. Our shared mission is to improve heart, lung, blood, and sleep health through basic science, translational, and clinical research.

## Scientific Information Officer, Aug 2022 - present

I manage a scientific IT team that enables the research of all NHLBI intramural labs. We collaborate directly with researchers to ensure that their scientific computing, networking, storage, and other IT needs enable their groundbreaking research. I work across the NIH's 27 Institutes and Centers to advocate for scientific IT infrastructre and policies that advance the NHLBI research mission.
