I worked in 3M's Corporate Research Systems Lab. We worked in cross functional teams spanning computer science, AI, electrical engineering, and mechanical engineering to research and develop new technologies and products across 3M's businesses.

## AI Research Manager, Aug 2021 - Aug 2022

I managed a group of about 20 AI and machine learning researchers who developed solutions for materials informatics, process informatics, generative design of materials, and clinical transcription.

## Software Research Manager, Feb 2019 - Aug 2021

I managed a group of about 15 software engineers and full stack developers who supported initiatives including cloud platform and application development, materials informatics, AI & IoT for manufacturing, and automated design.

## Data Scientist, Aug 2015 - Feb 2019

I spent my first three and a half years at 3M as a data scientist in the AI Lab. During that time I led and worked on projects for applications ranging from anti-counterfeiting to population health analytics to supply chain analysis. Some technologies I worked with included convultional deep neural networks, Tensorflow, Spark, word embeddings, and cloud services. As part of some of these projects, I collaborated with [Verily Life Sciences](https://verily.com) and [c3.ai](https://c3.ai).

## R&D Intern, Summer 2014

During my last summer of graduate school, I interned with the AI team. My intern projects focused on large scale data analysis of medical documentation using Map Reduce and anomaly detection of traffic flow data.
